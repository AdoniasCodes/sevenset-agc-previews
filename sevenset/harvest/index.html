Sesame seed

Sesamum indicum

Ethiopia is one of the world's largest sesame producers, and sesame is the country's leading oilseed export. We handle the three traded Ethiopian types.

  • HumeraWhite, high oil content. The premium Ethiopian grade, sought by tahini and confectionery buyers.
  • GondarWhitish, high oil content. Widely traded.
  • WollegaMixed reddish to brown colour. Used mainly for oil extraction.
