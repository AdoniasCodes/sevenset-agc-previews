Below ground

Earthworks and foundations.

Site clearance, bulk excavation, earthmoving, backfill, compaction and foundation solutions engineered for high rise and heavy industrial loading. Everything above ground depends on this, and nothing above ground can fix it.

What it takes

Why we survey before we price

  1. Site assessment first, so the ground is a known quantity and not a risk item.
  2. Design and technical computations against the actual conditions found.
  3. Testing and quality verification as layers are placed.
  4. Owned excavation and compaction plant, available when the formation is ready.
  5. One contractor from excavation through to the frame above it.

Addis Ababa ground conditions vary sharply across the city and sometimes across a single plot. We start with survey and site assessment before we price, not after we mobilise. A foundation redesign discovered in month two is the most expensive surprise a developer can be handed, and it is almost always avoidable.

Excavation, foundations and the structure that sits on them can all sit with one contractor. That removes the interface where a foundation contractor and a structural contractor each explain that the discrepancy is the other one's responsibility.

Foundations, structure and envelope under one contractor is the single biggest reason our programmes hold. It is the same reason the Megenagna envelope came in at four months.
