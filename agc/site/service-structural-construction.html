Grade One structural works

Structural construction.

Reinforced concrete frames, columns, slabs and cores for high rise and mid rise buildings across Ethiopia. Grade One is the only classification cleared for the country's largest structural contracts, and we have held it since 2018.

What it takes

How we hold a floor cycle

  1. Survey and set out before the first pour, not alongside it.
  2. Formwork booked as a cycle, so panels move up rather than sit waiting.
  3. Rebar delivered against the pour programme in batches.
  4. Concrete tested and recorded as it is placed, so a weak batch is caught in days, not at handover.
  5. Owned mixers and vibrators, so a pour is never waiting on a hire company.

Structure is where a programme is won or lost. A slab poured a week late does not cost a week. It costs every trade that was queued behind it, and those trades do not wait politely, they go to another site and come back when it suits them.

We plan the pour sequence, the formwork cycle and the rebar delivery as one schedule rather than three. That is the whole reason a floor cycle holds. It is not heroics on the day, it is the order in which the three things were booked six weeks earlier.

We took the structural and envelope work on a G+21 mixed use tower for Gift Real Estate, a 1.9 billion birr contract in Addis Ababa.

What is included.
