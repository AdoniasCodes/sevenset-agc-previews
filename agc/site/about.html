Grade four to grade one in five years.

Advanced General Contractors (AGC), formerly known in the market as 7Set General Trading, is a legally registered Ethiopian construction company operating at Grade One. The licence took five years and four upgrades. Nobody gives it to you.

The company

One contractor, the whole scope.

AGC delivers comprehensive construction services across specialized sectors: building solutions, road works, bridge and structural development, earthworks, foundation solutions, concrete and drainage systems, warehouse and industrial construction, water works, electrical and plumbing installations, steel and metal fabrication, finishing and renovation works, compound and external works, landscaping, equipment rental, and full project management.

With certified engineers, skilled technical teams and modern machinery, we execute both main contracts and subcontracted works, in full compliance with Ethiopian Construction Authority regulations.

The reason clients keep the whole scope with us is simple. Every interface between two contractors is a place where a programme can slip and where nobody is accountable. Fewer contractors means fewer of those places. The full list is on the services page.

Vision

To become one of Ethiopia's leading and most trusted construction contractors, delivering sustainable, innovative, high quality infrastructure that supports national development and positions the company among Africa's top ranked construction firms.

Mission

To provide client focused construction services with a strong commitment to excellence, safety, timely delivery and professional integrity, ensuring every project meets international standards and contributes to Ethiopia's growth.
