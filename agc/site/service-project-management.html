One point of accountability

Project management.

Full coordination and execution of a construction contract: planning, scheduling, supervision, quality control, procurement and safety management, on our own projects and on projects where we are engaged to run the work rather than build it.

What it takes

The seven stages, every time

  1. Surveying and site assessment.
  2. Design and technical computations.
  3. Testing and quality verification.
  4. Material preparation and mobilization.
  5. Human resource deployment.
  6. Construction and execution.
  7. Project completion and submission.

Modern machinery and project management software let us compress a programme without compressing the checks. That is the whole of what we mean by fast track quality. The four month Megenagna handover is the evidence rather than the claim.

Clients get one point of accountability, one programme and one set of reports. When something moves, they hear it from us before they see it on site. That is not a service feature, it is the minimum a client should expect and rarely gets.

The same sequence runs on a compound wall and on a 1.5 billion birr head office. The scale changes. The order does not.
