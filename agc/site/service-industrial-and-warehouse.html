Clear span and heavy duty

Industrial and warehouse.

Warehouses, industrial sheds, factory buildings and large clear span structures, including the steel fabrication and erection they depend on. We cut, weld and erect our own steel rather than subcontracting the part of the job that sets the programme.

What it takes

Operational, not just enclosed

  1. Clear span, loading and access agreed as design criteria at the start.
  2. Steel fabricated in house, so the critical path stays under our control.
  3. Services installed and commissioned as part of the contract.
  4. External works and access built for the vehicles that will actually use them.
  5. Handover against an operational test, not just a completed structure.

Industrial clients measure a building differently. Nobody is admiring the facade. What matters is the clear span, the floor loading, the door heights and the date the first truck can back in. We price and programme against those, and we say so in the proposal rather than discovering it at handover.

Water works, electrical and plumbing installation and external compound works all sit inside our Grade One scope. That means a warehouse can be handed over operational rather than merely enclosed, which is a different thing and the difference costs months.

Owned plant and in house fabrication are what let us compress an industrial programme without compressing the checks.
