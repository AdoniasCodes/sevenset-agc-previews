Signature service

Aluminum and glass works.

Aluminum doors and windows, glazing systems, architectural aluminum elements and sunbreakers, fabricated and installed to specification. One of the two scopes clients ask for us by name.

What it takes

Shop drawing to sealed opening

  1. Survey the structure as built before anything is fabricated.
  2. Shop drawings coordinated against that survey, not against the design intent alone.
  3. Fabrication in batches that follow the installation sequence.
  4. Installation, sealing and testing by the same team that fabricated it.
  5. Snagging closed out before handover rather than after it.

This is the reason main contractors bring us onto projects we are not building. We control the work from shop drawing through to a sealed, tested opening, which means there is no gap between the fabricator blaming the installer and the installer blaming the fabricator.

A facade package handled end to end by one party is a facade package that stays off a project's critical path. That is worth more to a main contractor than a lower unit rate, and the ones who have run a split package before already know it.

On the Transport Sectoral Head Office at Megenagna, the aluminum scope covered paneling and sunbreaker installation across the whole envelope, delivered inside a four month programme.
