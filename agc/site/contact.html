What happens next

Four steps, no chasing.

  1. 01

    We read it properly

    Someone technical reads your enquiry, not a sales desk. If the scope is unclear we will ask before quoting.

  2. 02

    A call within one working day

    To confirm scale, location, programme and whether we are the right contractor for it. Sometimes we are not, and we will say so.

  3. 03

    Site visit and assessment

    Survey and site assessment, which is step one of how we run every project, tender or not.

  4. 04

    A proposal with a date on it

    Scope, method, programme and price. The date in our proposal is the date we intend to hand over.
