Where programmes are lost

Interiors and fit-out.

Interior partition systems, space division, ceilings, surface finishes, detailing and renovation works, coordinated with building services so that ducts, conduit and partitions land where the drawings say.

What it takes

Planned as its own project

  1. Services coordination resolved on drawings before partitions go up.
  2. Trade sequence set so no trade is waiting on two others at once.
  3. Quality checked room by room as it completes, not in one pass at the end.
  4. Snag list opened early and closed before handover.
  5. One party accountable for the whole interior, not five.

Finishing carries the most trades, the most interfaces and the least float, and it is usually the scope that gets planned last. That combination is why most programmes are lost here rather than in the structure.

We plan fit-out as its own project with its own sequence. It is the only reason a four month envelope and fit out programme is possible at all. It is also the scope a client looks at every day for the next twenty years, so snagging and handover are part of the contract rather than a favour after it.

Finishing works across the whole Megenagna building ran in parallel with the envelope rather than behind it. That is what four months actually required.
