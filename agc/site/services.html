All types of construction.

AGC holds Grade One (GC-1), the top tier of the Ethiopian Construction Authority classification. That licence is not a specialism. It is the legal and technical mandate to take on any category of construction work in the country, at any scale, as main contractor or subcontractor.

Where we are asked for by name

Four signature specializations.

Contractors call us in for these four when the envelope has to perform and the programme has no room. They are also the scopes that carried the Ministry of Transport building to handover in four months.

01Specialization

Aluminum and Glass Works

Aluminum doors and windows, glazing systems, and architectural elements fabricated and installed to specification. We control the work from shop drawing to sealed, tested opening, which is what keeps a facade package off a project's critical path.

  • Aluminum doors and windows
  • Glazing systems
  • Architectural elements
  • Sunbreakers
  • Shop drawings
02Specialization

Curtain Wall and Facade Works

Curtain wall systems engineered for structural integrity, weather resistance, and long term performance, not just for the day of handover. This is the most visible thing a building does and the least forgiving to get wrong.

  • Unitised and stick systems
  • Weather and water testing
  • Structural sealing
  • Aluminum paneling
  • Cladding
03Specialization

Partition and Interior Works

Interior partition systems and space division, coordinated with building services so that ceilings, ducts, conduit and partitions land where the drawings say and not where the last trade left room.

  • Partition systems
  • Space division
  • Services coordination
  • Ceilings
04Specialization

Finishing and Fit-Out Works

Final interior completion, surface finishes and detailing. Finishing is where a programme is usually lost, because it is the scope with the most trades, the most interfaces and the least float. We plan it as its own project.

  • Surface finishes
  • Detailing
  • Renovation works
  • Snagging and handover

Owned, not hired

The machinery is ours.

Plant that belongs to the contractor does not queue behind another client's programme. AGC owns and maintains its own fleet, kept in on-site ready condition rather than called off when a milestone is already late.

Fabrication: metal cutting machines, grinding machines, welding machines, drilling machines.
Haulage and production: dump trucks, pick-up trucks, hollow block machines, stone aggregate crushers.
