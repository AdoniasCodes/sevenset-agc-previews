All types of construction.

Advanced General Contractors holds Grade One (GC-1), the top tier of the Ethiopian Construction Authority classification. That licence is not a specialism. It is the legal and technical mandate to take on any category of construction work in Ethiopia, at any scale, as main contractor or as subcontractor.

01Service

Structural Construction

Reinforced concrete frames, columns, slabs and cores for high rise and mid rise buildings in Addis Ababa and across Ethiopia. Grade One is the only classification cleared to take on the country's largest structural contracts, and it is the grade we have held since 2018.

Structure is where a programme is won or lost. A slab poured a week late does not cost a week, it costs every trade that was queued behind it. We plan the pour sequence, the formwork cycle and the rebar delivery as one schedule rather than three, which is how a floor cycle actually holds.

We work as main contractor on our own projects and as structural subcontractor to other main contractors, including on the Transport Sectoral Head Office and a G+21 mixed use tower for Gift Real Estate.

  • Reinforced concrete frames
  • Formwork and falsework
  • Rebar fixing
  • Slabs and cores
  • High rise structures

02Service

Roads and Bridges

Road construction, upgrading, asphalt surfacing, bridge works and structural development for public infrastructure. This is the work that put us in front of government clients, and it is why Grade One mattered to us before any private developer had heard the name.

Road contracts live or die on plant availability. Ours is owned rather than hired, which means a compactor does not arrive three days after the subgrade was ready. Dump trucks, plate compactors, aggregate crushers and mixers are on our books and on our maintenance schedule.

Public infrastructure also carries a documentation burden that private work does not. Test results, method statements and quality records are produced as the work happens, not assembled the week before a certificate is due.

  • Road construction and upgrading
  • Asphalt and surfacing
  • Bridge works
  • Drainage and culverts
  • Public infrastructure

03Service

Earthworks and Foundations

Site clearance, bulk excavation, earthmoving, backfill, compaction and foundation solutions engineered for high rise and heavy industrial loading. Everything above ground depends on this being right, and nothing above ground can fix it if it is not.

Addis Ababa ground conditions vary sharply across the city and even across a single plot. We start with survey and site assessment before we price, not after we mobilise, because a foundation redesign discovered in month two is the most expensive kind of surprise a developer can be handed.

Excavation, foundations and the structure that sits on them can all sit with one contractor. That removes the interface where a foundation contractor and a structural contractor each say the other is responsible for the discrepancy.

  • Site clearance
  • Bulk excavation
  • Backfill and compaction
  • Foundation solutions
  • Concrete and drainage

04Service

Industrial and Warehouse

Warehouses, industrial sheds, factory buildings and large clear span structures, including the steel fabrication and erection they depend on. We cut, weld and erect our own steel rather than subcontracting the part of the job that sets the programme.

Industrial clients measure a building differently. Nobody is admiring the facade. What matters is the clear span, the floor loading, the door heights and the date the first truck can back in. We price and programme against those, and we say so in the proposal.

Water works, electrical and plumbing installation and external compound works are all inside our Grade One scope, so a warehouse can be handed over operational rather than merely enclosed.

  • Warehouses and industrial sheds
  • Clear span structures
  • Steel and metal fabrication
  • Water works
  • Electrical and plumbing

Aluminum and Glass Works

Aluminum doors and windows, glazing systems, architectural aluminum elements and sunbreakers, fabricated and installed to specification. This is one of the two scopes clients ask for us by name, and it is the reason main contractors bring us onto projects we are not building.

We control the work from shop drawing through to a sealed, tested opening. A facade package handled end to end by one party is a facade package that stays off a project's critical path, because there is no gap between the fabricator blaming the installer and the installer blaming the fabricator.

On the Ministry of Transport building at Megenagna the aluminum scope covered paneling and sunbreaker installation across the whole envelope, delivered inside a four month programme.

  • Aluminum doors and windows
  • Glazing systems
  • Architectural aluminum
  • Sunbreakers

06Signature service

Curtain Walls and Facades

Curtain wall and facade systems engineered for structural integrity, weather resistance and long term performance. Unitised and stick systems, structural sealing, aluminum paneling and cladding, with weather and water testing as part of the work rather than as an argument afterwards.

A facade is the most visible thing a building does and the least forgiving to get wrong. A sealed joint that fails shows up as a stain on the third floor within one rainy season, and by then the contractor has usually gone. We test before we hand over.

Setting out is the hidden difficulty. A curtain wall is installed against a structure that is already built, with whatever tolerance that structure actually achieved rather than whatever the drawing promised. Surveying that honestly, early, is what keeps the panels landing on line.

  • Unitised and stick systems
  • Structural sealing
  • Weather and water testing
  • Aluminum paneling
  • Cladding

07Service

Interiors and Fit-Out

Interior partition systems, space division, ceilings, surface finishes, detailing and renovation works, coordinated with building services so that ducts, conduit and partitions land where the drawings say rather than where the last trade left room.

Finishing is where most programmes are lost. It carries the most trades, the most interfaces and the least float, and it is usually the scope that gets planned last. We plan it as its own project with its own sequence, which is the only reason a four month envelope and fit out programme is possible at all.

Fit-out is also the scope a client sees every day for the next twenty years. Snagging and handover are part of the contract, not a favour after it.

  • Partition systems
  • Ceilings and services coordination
  • Surface finishes and detailing
  • Renovation works
  • Snagging and handover

08Service

Project Management

Full coordination and execution of a construction contract: planning, scheduling, supervision, quality control, procurement and safety management, on our own projects and on projects where we are engaged to run the work rather than to build it.

Modern machinery and project management software let us compress a programme without compressing the checks. That is the whole of what we mean by fast track quality, and the four month Megenagna handover is the evidence rather than the claim.

Clients get one point of accountability, one programme and one set of reports. When something moves, they hear it from us before they see it on site.
