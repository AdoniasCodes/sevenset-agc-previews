About the orange dashed boxes

Anything inside an orange dashed box is a placeholder waiting on information from the business. They are marked clearly on purpose so nothing unverified is ever presented as fact.

  • For Seven S ET: the product specifications (purity, moisture, oil content, packing, minimum order), the year the company started, the actual export destinations, the export licence number and TIN, and the machinery import division.
  • For AGC: a few remaining figures and the final brand files.

The rest is real: the products and grades, the company's own mission and vision, the quality process, the delivered projects, and the contact details.

About the photography

Every photograph in these previews is a temporary stand in, not a picture of the actual business. They are there to show the shape and rhythm of the page. Replacing them with real photography of the sites, the warehouse, the grading line and the team is one of the biggest single improvements available, and it is worth planning a short shoot before launch.
